# About

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Jae-Joon Kim.

My research interests are VLSI design and efficient AI, with a focus on numerical formats and hardware acceleration for efficient LLM inference and fine-tuning. My experience also includes digital VLSI design for computing-in-memory systems, tape-out, and chip measurement.

[Email](mailto:Jehun.lee@snu.ac.kr) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=3xR-zbMAAAAJ)

## Research Interests

- Efficient AI and low-precision numerical formats
- LLM hardware acceleration
- Digital VLSI design and computing-in-memory systems
